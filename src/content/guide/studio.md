# Shows, people, and settings

This page is the workspace around a single skit: a series, the people in it, and the defaults every new brief inherits. Owners change settings. Editors make the films. Clients review.

## Projects

Projects is the home page. The count under the title is how many shorts match the filters.

**New short** starts a brief. **New show** starts a series. Both sit at the top right. On an empty workspace the only button is **New short**, in the middle of the page.

The search box is **Search titles and lines**. Under it:

- **All types** filters to Stick-figure skit, or to the other kind of short this studio can make.
- **All steps** filters by Script, Edit, Review, or Export. A stick-figure skit uses Script, Review, and Export. Edit belongs to the other kind.
- **Needs you** shows projects with comments still open.
- **Ready to post** shows ones that can go out.
- **Archived** shows archived rows, and hides the live ones.
- **Mine** shows projects you created or last touched.
- **Clear** resets all of that.

A row shows the title, the kind, the step, and when it was touched. Click the title to open it on the step it has reached.

**More** on a row:

| Item | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Rename | The title becomes a box. Type, then press Enter. |
| Duplicate | A second project with the same script. |
| 3 variations | Asks for three more drafts of this skit. The button on a show does the same for a topic you type. The confirm box states the price, three script writes, up to $0.06. Nothing is voiced. The new ones stay archived until you open one. Turn on **Archived** to see them. |
| Archive / Unarchive | Hides it from the normal list, or brings it back. |
| Delete | Asks you to confirm. The confirm says it cannot be undone. |

Shows sit above the filters. **Open** goes to the show. **New episode** starts a brief with that show’s format, cast, set, and tone already chosen.

**Reviews** in the header is the queue of comments across projects. The number is how many are waiting. The page title is **Waiting on you**.

The pill next to the header is the connection. **Tamtree connected** is what you want. Anything else means new work is not really being written. That is fixed on [How the studio is wired](/guide/running-it), not by retrying the brief.

## A show

**New show** asks for a name, a format, two cast slots, a set, a tone, hashtags, the AI-voice line (it starts as “Voices are AI-generated.”), and a limit. Save it and you return to Projects.

Open the show.

**Ideas** is a list, one idea per line. Type them, press **Add**. **↑** reorders. **×** removes one. When an idea has a draft, **Draft** opens that script.

**A week** is how many shorts you intend. The title line shows how many of that number exist this week, for example “1 of 3 this week”.

**Draft ahead** writes upcoming scripts up to the weekly cap, at the script price each, and voices nothing until you approve. **Weekly cap** is how many of those drafts it may write. Press **Save cadence** after you change the numbers. **Write the next drafts now** runs that pass immediately. It only shows when draft ahead is on.

**Brand** is the look of captions on the preview.

1. Pick a **Font**.
2. Set **Caption**, **Highlight**, and **Accent** colours.
3. Pick a **Position**.
4. **Logo URL** is optional. Choose **Watermark** or **End card**.
5. **End card** is the line, such as “Follow for part 2”, and a length of 1.5 or 2 seconds.
6. Press **Save brand**.

New episodes inherit it. The preview on the script and on export shows it on the phone. The note under the button is accurate: burning that frame into the downloaded film is not happening yet.

**How the posts did** fills in after a post is live and the connector reports views. Until then the page says views show up after a post is live. TikTok says “Stats after TikTok audit”. When rows exist, each episode links to its export, with views, percent watched, and likes.

## Settings

Settings is in the header. A client who opens it sees “Settings stay with the workspace owner.” An editor can look. Only the owner can change the stick-figure section. The form says so at the bottom if you are not the owner.

### Invite

Owners only. Type an email, pick **Editor** or **Client**, press **Invite**.

Editors make skits. Clients can review and approve. Clients do not see cost or settings. The line under the button says that.

### Notifications

Tick the ones you want, then **Save notifications**.

- A client commented
- A client approved
- A film is ready
- A post went live
- Also send these to Slack

**Slack webhook** is the owner’s. Paste the `https://hooks.slack.com/…` address and press **Save Slack**.

### Your cast

This adds a character of your own. It does not replace Milo, June, and the others.

The form starts from a house body, then you set the name, colours, hair, height, head size, limbs, and a personality line. The personality line is what the writer reads. Tick the original-characters box when the design must not be a real person’s likeness. Save it, and it shows up in the cast gallery on the next brief.

**Remove** deletes one you added.

There is also a voice-consent control for the owner’s own cloned voice. Leave it off unless you have actually agreed to that. Export’s AI-voice line changes when it is on.

### Stick-figure skit

This block is what every new brief is allowed to use.

**Characters and their voices.** Each row is a person, on or off, a **Sample** button, a pitch slider, a pace slider, and a voice menu. Pitch and pace run from 0.5 to 2. Sample says “This is a sample line.” in that voice, at that pitch and pace. Turn someone off and new briefs cannot pick them. Existing projects keep the cast they already have.

The voice menu is the eight voices, described the way the studio lists them:

| Voice | How it is described |
| --- | --- |
| Puck | Bright, upbeat |
| Kore | Firm, even |
| Zephyr | Warm, light |
| Charon | Deep, steady |
| Fenrir | Low, dry |
| Aoede | Clear, quick |
| Leda | Soft, close |
| Orus | Even, warm |

Give every character who is turned on a voice. The page will not save a cast with a hole there.

**Sets.** Press a room to allow it. The caption counts how many of the sixteen are on. A brief can only pick from the ones left on.

**Starting format.** **Writer’s pick**, or one format that new briefs open on.

**Hashtags on every caption.** Optional, up to 200 characters. They are appended on export. `#skit #comedy` is the shape the box suggests.

**Spend cap per video.** A dollar amount. A video’s own limit starts here and can never be raised past it on the brief. The default is 1.00.

Press **Save**. The status line says **Saved. New briefs start from these.** A refusal names the field. “Allow at least one set.” and “Allow at least one character.” mean you turned the last one off.

Changing settings does not rewrite a skit you already approved. It changes the next brief.

## A week of work, in order

1. Owner: voices, allowed sets, cap, hashtags, invites. Once.
2. Owner or editor: a show, with a format and a cast, if these skits are a series.
3. Ideas on the show, one line each.
4. **New episode** for the one you will make today.
5. Script, approve, review link, export.
6. Look at **Needs you** the next morning before you write another.
