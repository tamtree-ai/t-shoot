# Export and post

Export is the finished file and the words that go with it. Open it from the top bar once **Version 1 is ready** on the script. If you open it sooner, the page says **No video yet. Approve the skit to make one.** and offers **Back to the skit**.

## Play the cut you will publish

The player is on the left. **Version** in the corner switches cuts when you have more than one. The line under the player is the size (1080×1920 once the file has loaded), the length, the version number, the file size, and who approved it.

A show can put a brand frame around this preview: caption colour, a logo, an end card. That frame is on the preview. Burning the brand into the file itself waits. If the downloaded MP4 does not show the logo, that is the current behaviour, not a failed download. The brand controls are on the show, described in [Shows, people, and settings](/guide/studio).

## Take the files

On the right, three downloads:

| Button | File | Use it for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Download MP4 | The video | The post, the ad, the client delivery |
| Captions | Subtitles, .srt | A platform that wants a separate caption file. The words match the script. |
| Post text | A .txt | Description, hashtags, the AI-voice note, and the script, in one file |

Under those:

- **Copy caption** copies the description.
- **Copy hashtags** copies the tags. Tags you set on the stick-figure settings are included.
- **Copy AI-voice line** copies the disclosure. With no custom line, it reads “Voices are AI-generated (text-to-speech).” If the owner has agreed to a cloned voice, the line also says the owner’s voice was cloned with their consent. Paste this next to the post. Do not drop it.
- **Save cover frame**, or **Use a different frame** when a cover already exists. Scrub the film to the picture you want, then press it. It downloads a JPEG named with the title and the version. A generated cover, when the render included one, is the default. Replace it only when you want a different frame.
- **Copy review link** if this version already has one. Otherwise **Share for review** opens Review so you can create it.

The caption and the hashtags are also printed on the panel, so you can read them before you copy.

## Prepare a post

Open **Prepare a post — this does not upload**.

Read the line under the heading. Saving and confirming do not upload. **Post** is the button that sends.

1. Pick **YouTube**, **Instagram**, or **TikTok**.
2. Check **Title**, **Caption**, and **Hashtags**. They start from the skit. Edit them here. Editing does not change the film.
3. On YouTube, set **Tags** (comma separated) and **Category**. Comedy is the usual one. People & Blogs, Entertainment, and Film & Animation are the others.
4. Set **Privacy**: Private, Unlisted, or Public.
5. Leave **Label as AI-generated** ticked. The voices are generated.
6. **Schedule** is optional. Set a date and time, or leave it empty to mean “when I press Post”.

Two limits you should know before you promise a public post:

- YouTube uploads stay private until Google’s audit, even if you picked Public.
- TikTok is handed off as a draft. You confirm privacy in TikTok. Stats for TikTok stay empty until that audit, and the show page says so.

**Save draft** stores the form. The note says **Draft saved.**

**Confirm** checks the form and marks it confirmed. The note says **Confirmed. Nothing has been uploaded.** Confirm stays unavailable while the form has a problem. The problem is printed in amber above the buttons. Title, caption, hashtags, and tags each have a length limit. YouTube’s title is 100 characters. Captions allow up to 5,000 on YouTube and 2,200 on Instagram and TikTok.

**Post** stays grey until the form is confirmed. Press it to send. The result line is either the live URL, or the failure. **Confirmed. Not live yet.** means you confirmed and have not posted, or the upload has not reported back.

A confirmed form that you then edit drops back to a draft. Confirm again before Post.

## Versions

The list at the bottom is every cut: number, length, date. Press a row to load it into the player and the buttons. Download from the row you mean. The MP4 of version 1 does not become version 2.

If a version shows an amber reminder, read it before you post. It is a note the render attached to that cut.
